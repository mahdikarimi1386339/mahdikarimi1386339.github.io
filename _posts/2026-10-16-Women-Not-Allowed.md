I came up with a very hilarious proverb in English, and it is really just for me. I am not the kind of person who laughs at his own jokes a lot, but I do laugh at them sometimes. I guess proverbs can stem from our inner desires. People could not speak of them in their own words, so they blamed their ancestors for what they had not even done or thought about.  
  
This morning, I was thinking about how awful it is to talk to yourself about sexuality. You are surrounded by it, and you will not even believe it. After thinking, I reached a conclusion: when I am not sexually active, I prefer women, but I don't think I am a pervert, because the veil of chastity makes my ego stronger to refuse the existence of sexual desire.  
  
When a man is deeply immersed in sexuality, people will think that he is a pervert who wants to sleep with everyone, but I believe that the more pure you are in practice, the more lecherous you are deep down.  
  
Finally, this is the proverb I have made in English: "Masturbating two times a day keeps the girls away." :)
